UPS — Delivery Route Optimization

The ORION System: how Machine Learning reshaped one of the world's largest delivery networks

Problem Context

A global logistics company needs to reduce the time and cost of its daily deliveries. With thousands of drivers and millions of stops per day, even small inefficiencies in route planning translate into massive fuel and time losses.

Data Used

Delivery addresses, traffic conditions, schedules, and route constraints.

What It Predicts

The optimal route by minimizing left turns, reducing accident risk and delivery time.

Type of ML

Supervised Learning / data-driven optimization based on historical data.

Benefit

Saves millions of dollars in fuel costs and enables faster deliveries.
