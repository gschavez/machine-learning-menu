Use Case 2: UPS — Delivery Route Optimization (ORION System)

Context: a global logistics company needs to reduce the time and cost of its daily deliveries.

Data used: delivery addresses, traffic conditions, schedules, route constraints.

What the model predicts/discovers: the optimal route by minimizing left turns, reducing accident risk and delivery time.

Type of Machine Learning: Supervised Learning / data-driven optimization based on historical data.

Benefit: saves millions of dollars in fuel costs and enables faster deliveries.
